What’s the difference between front-end, back-end, and full-stack?

Front-end focuses on the user-facing interface. Back-end covers server-side logic, APIs, and data. The Full-stack Development course combines both areas; the front-end and back-end courses are also available separately.

What technologies do you teach?

Front-end topics include HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Back-end topics include Node.js and Express, with database fundamentals using MongoDB and PostgreSQL. Mobile app tools are agreed to fit the learning goals.

What are the course fees?

Front-end: ₦180,000; Back-end: ₦200,000; Full-stack: ₦550,000; Mobile App Development: ₦600,000. The four-week 1:1 Mentorship program is ₦600,000.

Do I need prior coding experience?

Tell me what you’ve learned so far and what you want to achieve. We’ll agree the right starting point and session scope before confirming a program.

How long does a course take?

Course schedules are agreed based on the topic and learning goals. The 1:1 Mentorship program runs for four weeks and includes four private 60-minute sessions.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions can be delivered online or in person by arrangement. For in-person sessions, location, travel, and venue costs are confirmed in the proposal.

Will I get hands-on practice?

Yes. Programs include guided practice, exercises, and feedback as described in the selected package. Project guidance is included in the Full-stack and Mentorship tracks.

What should I include when enquiring?

Share your chosen topic, current experience, learning goals, preferred schedule, and whether you’re enquiring for yourself or a team. I’ll recommend the next step.
