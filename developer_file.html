QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you
start a project.

Still have a question? Call or connect on LinkedIn and tell me what you’re planning.

What’s included in each website package?

Each package lists its included deliverables. The Launch package covers a one-page site; Business covers up to five custom-designed pages; Custom is scoped around your project. The final proposal confirms the exact work.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Responsive development is included in the packages so the agreed website can adapt to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

How long will my project take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope, content readiness, feedback, and any integrations. I’ll include a project schedule in the proposal before work begins.

Are domain registration and hosting included?

Third-party costs such as domains and hosting are separate unless they are specifically listed in your proposal. Any provider costs are confirmed before you commit.

Can you redesign an existing website or add custom features?

Share your current site and what you want to improve. I’ll review the requirements and confirm whether the work fits a package or needs a custom quote.

How do payments work?

The booking deposit and any milestone payment plan are confirmed in your proposal before the project starts.

Do you provide support after launch?

Each project includes its agreed testing and handover. Ongoing maintenance or changes after launch can be discussed and quoted separately.

What should I share to get a quote?

Tell me about your business, audience, goals, required pages or features, target launch date, and any existing domain or brand materials. I’ll recommend a scope and next step.
